# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name
Aerie (by American Eagle Outfitters)

## Industry
Retail / Apparel & Intimates

## Tone of Voice
- Empathetic and unconditionally supportive
- Casual and peer-to-peer (like a close friend, never a rigid corporate entity)
- Authentic and transparent, matching the #AerieREAL ethosJoyful, uplifting, and body-positive

## Target Audience
- Gen Z and Millennial women aged 15–35
- Individuals seeking everyday comfort over high-fashion restriction
- Value-driven consumers who prioritize body inclusivity and real representation in media

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. #AerieREAL: Real power, real beauty, and zero digital retouching.
2. Effortless Comfort: Designing intimates and activewear made to move with you, exactly as you are.Inclusivity
3. First: Welcoming all body types, abilities, backgrounds, and identities into our community.

## Topics to Avoid
- Weight loss, dieting, or "body transformation" narratives
- Airbrushing, digital smoothing, or modifying physical skin textures and body shapes
- High-fashion exclusivity or promoting unrealistic, restrictive beauty standards
- Hyper-sexualized marketing or rigid editorial posing
- Competitor bashing or body-shaming terminology
